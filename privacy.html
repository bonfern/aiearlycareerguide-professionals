Privacy Policy

Professional assessment · Last updated October 3, 2026

1. Information we collect

Your email address, purchase and coupon records, profile answers, selected assessment answers, assessment-session identifiers and report data. Technical logs may include standard service and fraud-prevention information.

2. How information is used

Your email is used to deliver the assessment report and provide transaction or assessment support, not automatically to subscribe you to marketing. Profile and assessment answers are used to create and store the report.

3. Service providers

We use Razorpay to process payments, Firebase/Firestore to store assessment and order records, OpenAI to help generate assessment content and reports, Resend to deliver report emails, and Vercel to host the application. Only the data needed for these purposes should be sent to each provider.

4. Shared question banks

General question banks for similar roles may be reused across users. Your individual answers and personal report are kept in your own assessment session and are not included in a shared question bank.

5. Security and retention

Access to your assessment uses a secure session token. Browser-based access periods do not automatically delete all server records. We retain data to deliver reports and resolve support, payment or compliance issues; access and deletion requests can be sent to the contact below. The site's separate 30-day Firestore deletion policy, if enabled, governs applicable assessment records.

6. Your choices

Use only personal information you are authorised to share. Avoid confidential employer, client or third-party information. You may request information about your stored data or ask for deletion subject to legal and transaction-record retention requirements.

7. Contact

Email info@aiearlycareerguide.com for privacy and data requests.
