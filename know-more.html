CAREER GUIDE FOR PROFESSIONALS

About the Assessment

AI Early Career Guide for Professionals helps adults assess the essential skills required for a chosen role or career goal. It offers practical guidance rather than a promise of employment.

Who is the assessment for?

Working professionals planning growth or promotion, career changers, people returning to work, recent graduates and entrepreneurs exploring a new direction.

How it works

  1. Enter your email address and optionally apply a coupon.
  2. Pay the displayed price (₹499 before any discount), or redeem a valid complimentary coupon.
  3. Answer simple profile questions appropriate to your career stage.
  4. Complete short multiple-choice scenarios covering the essential technical and behavioural skills for your stated goal.
  5. Receive a personalised report by email and view it on the completion screen.

What does the report contain?

Your target-role skill requirements; test-based indications of your current competency; identified gaps and specific development actions; important skills not assessed in this focused test; and a 30/60/90-day plan.

What does the assessment not measure?

Multiple-choice performance does not independently verify workplace performance, qualifications, credentials or suitability for a specific vacancy. Requirements vary among employers. Use the report alongside actual job descriptions, verified experience and professional feedback.
