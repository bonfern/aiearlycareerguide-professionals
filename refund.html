1. Who can use the guarantee?

The guarantee applies to a paid Career Guide for Professionals assessment after its personalised report has first been generated or emailed. Submit the request within 7 calendar days of that date.

2. How to request a refund

  1. Email us from the purchase emailUse the refund button below or email info@aiearlycareerguide.com.
  2. Provide the transaction referenceInclude the Razorpay payment ID or order ID when available. This is used only to locate the correct payment.
  3. Receive acknowledgementWe aim to acknowledge the request within 1 business day.
  4. Refund initiationAfter matching the transaction and confirming that it has not already been refunded, we aim to initiate the full refund within 2 business days.
  5. ConfirmationWe will email the refund reference. The time for the credit to appear depends on the original payment method, payment provider and bank.
Request a Refund →Contact Support

3. No-questions-asked means no explanation is required

We will not ask you to explain or prove why the report did not meet your expectations. We may verify the purchase email, transaction reference, amount paid, report date and whether the transaction has already been refunded. These checks protect customers from an incorrect or duplicate refund.

4. Discounted and complimentary assessments

For a discounted purchase, the refund is 100% of the amount actually paid. A complimentary assessment obtained through a 100% discount has no paid amount to refund.

5. Duplicate charges, failed access or technical failures

If money was debited but the assessment did not unlock, do not pay again. Use Check Previous Payment at checkout and contact us if the issue remains. Duplicate payments and unrecoverable technical failures are handled separately and do not require completion of the report or a statement of dissatisfaction.

6. Refund method

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method wherever supported. One refund may be issued for each paid transaction, and a transaction that has already been fully refunded cannot be refunded again.

7. Statutory rights

This policy does not limit mandatory protections available under applicable consumer law.
