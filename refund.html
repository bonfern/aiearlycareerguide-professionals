Refund & Cancellation Policy

Professional assessment · Last updated October 3, 2026

1. Complimentary assessments

A 100% discount coupon provides a complimentary assessment; there is no payment to refund.

2. Paid assessments

Because access to this digital service begins immediately after successful payment verification, refunds are generally not offered after assessment access has been issued or the assessment has started, subject to applicable consumer rights and the exceptions below.

3. Payment debited but assessment not unlocked

Do not pay again. Use the checkout's Check Previous Payment feature. If you were debited but your payment is shown as failed or unresolved, contact us with the payment reference; we'll check Razorpay records, the payment provider's status and whether access or a refund is appropriate.

4. Duplicate payments or technical failures

Contact us if you are charged twice, receive no access after a successful payment, or encounter an unrecoverable service error. We will verify the transaction and provide appropriate access restoration or refund where warranted.

5. Discounted purchases

Refunds, if applicable, are based on the amount actually paid rather than the undiscounted price.

6. Requesting help

Email info@aiearlycareerguide.com from your purchase email, including the payment ID and a brief description. Never send your API keys or full card details.

7. Statutory rights

This policy does not limit mandatory protections under applicable consumer law.
